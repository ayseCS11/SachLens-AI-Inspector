What it does

Take a photo of a poster, a letter or an offer. SachLens reads it on your phone and marks the red flags: fees asked first, guaranteed visas, impossible dates, payment receipts inside offer letters, QR codes that lead to a different website, and more. Every warning shows its evidence and tells you how to confirm it yourself.

Try it in 3 minutes

  1. Download the APK on an Android phone and install it (see the help below).
  2. Open the app, tap Check a poster, and photograph or pick a poster that says something like "Dubai jobs, guaranteed visa, pay a registration fee first". The red flags appear as numbered boxes on the poster.
  3. Go back and use Pick an image on an offer letter. Try a letter that is dated before a payment it claims to confirm.
  4. Optional: turn on airplane mode and scan again. Nothing needs the internet.

Sample files and test cases are in the repository.

If the phone blocks the install

  • Open the downloaded file. When Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps.
  • If Play Protect shows a warning, choose "More details", then "Install anyway". The app is signed by its developer and is not yet on the Play Store.

What it checks

  • Scam wording in English, Roman Urdu, Spanish, French and Portuguese
  • Dates and timelines, totals that do not add up, impossible scores
  • File details: editing software, AI tool labels, camera data
  • Edited areas of a photo (error level analysis) and a side by side compare mode
  • QR codes and links, checked as text without opening them
  • Batch scan, PDF report, and a privacy cleaner for photos

Honest limits

SachLens finds warning signs. It cannot prove that a document is genuine, and it cannot see a careful forgery that has no contradictions. It cannot read Urdu or Arabic script yet, and it does not use a generative AI model to guess whether a picture is AI made. It says so on screen and tells you to confirm with the issuer.

Privacy

Files are checked on your phone. The core checks need no account, no server and no paid service.
