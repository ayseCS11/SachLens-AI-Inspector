01

Overview

SachLens (“SachLens”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is an AI-assisted document and image inspection application developed and provided by Tekno Efsun SMC (Pvt.) Ltd.

People use SachLens to look for potential signs of image manipulation, AI-generated imagery, altered document fields, and inconsistencies in digital content. Its results are indicators for further review, not official verification or a guarantee of authenticity.

This Policy describes information that may be processed when you use the app. The exact information handled depends on the features enabled in the version you use.

02

Information we handle

Files you choose

Images, document files, screenshots, or other supported content you deliberately select for analysis.

Analysis results

Findings, scores, extracted text, heat-map data, and reports generated by features you use.

Technical information

Basic device, app version, diagnostic, or crash information only if the app or its integrated services collect it.

Messages to us

Contact details and the content you provide if you email us or request support.

We do not ask you to submit a document unless you choose to use a feature that requires it. Files may contain personal or sensitive information, such as names, identification numbers, financial details, academic records, or photographs. Please inspect only content you are authorized to use.

Do not assume that every category above is collected. The categories actually processed depend on the app's implementation, permissions, and enabled SDKs. The publisher should remove any category that does not apply and disclose any additional collection.

03

How analysis works

SachLens may use a combination of on-device processing and optional external services, depending on the released build and the feature selected.

On-device analysis

Where implemented, the app may inspect file properties, compare image pixels, calculate file fingerprints, run image-forensic checks, extract text, or evaluate local consistency rules directly on your device. Information processed only locally is not transmitted to us by that processing step.

External AI or online services

If a feature sends an image, document, extracted text, or analysis details to an AI provider or other online service, the relevant information is transmitted to that provider for the requested operation. The provider may process it under its own terms and privacy policy. The app should clearly disclose such processing before it occurs, where required.

Important: Do not upload confidential or sensitive documents to an external service unless you are authorized to do so and understand how that service handles the information.

Automated assessments

Analysis results may include suspected editing, possible AI-generation indicators, or inconsistencies. Automated systems can make mistakes. SachLens does not independently establish that a person committed fraud, that a document is legally invalid, or that a claim shown in a screenshot is true.

04

Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. Information may be shared only where the actual app functionality requires it or where legally permitted, including the following situations if applicable:

  • AI, OCR, or analysis providers: information you submit to an enabled external service to perform a requested feature.
  • Infrastructure and diagnostics providers: limited technical information processed by services integrated into the app, if any.
  • Your chosen destination: a report or file you explicitly export, share, or send using your device's sharing features.
  • Legal and safety requirements: information that must be disclosed to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and policies. The publisher should list the providers actually used by the production app and link to their current privacy policies before release.

05

Storage and retention

Retention depends on where information is processed and which features you use.

  • Temporary processing: files processed in memory may remain available while analysis is running and until the app or operating system releases that memory.
  • Local files and reports: exported reports or files you save may remain on your device until you delete them.
  • External providers: if data is sent to a third-party service, that provider's retention practices and contractual terms may apply.
  • Support and diagnostics: correspondence or diagnostic records may be retained as needed to resolve issues, maintain security, or meet legal obligations, if such records are collected.

We should not claim that all temporary files are automatically deleted unless the app has been tested to confirm that behavior across supported devices and file types.

06

Device permissions

SachLens may request access to the photo picker, file picker, camera, or other device capabilities when needed for a feature. The actual permissions depend on the installed version and the operating system.

You can manage app permissions in your device settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working, but should not affect unrelated functionality where technically possible.

07

Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. Processing data on-device, where available, can reduce the need to transmit sensitive files.

No application, device, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device updated, protect access to saved reports, and avoid sharing sensitive files through destinations you do not trust.

08

Children's privacy

SachLens is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without the required authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support channel, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action where required by law.

The publisher must set the app's target audience and age rating accurately in Google Play Console.

09

Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Information stored only on your device can generally be removed by deleting the relevant file, report, or app data. Information handled by a third-party provider may need to be managed through that provider's procedures.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details in the Contact section. We may need to verify your request before responding.
